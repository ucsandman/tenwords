# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person (the developer) on their own machine, condensing walls of text they didn't write: AI chat output, long articles, PDFs. They arrive mid-task with something verbose on the clipboard and want the gist in seconds, then leave. Used in short bursts, often several times a day, on a desktop browser.

## Product Purpose

TenWords turns any text into exactly-ten-word paragraph summaries plus a ten-word TL;DR. It exists to kill jargon-wading: paste, condense, skim, move on. Success is the user trusting the summaries enough to stop reading originals.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, literary, exact. The interface reads like a well-set book page, not an app: serif type, generous whitespace, one calm column. The ten-word constraint is the personality; the design's job is to frame it without ceremony.

## Anti-references

- The cream/parchment "AI-generated 2026" look: warm near-white backgrounds, `--paper`/`--linen` token energy. Neutrals must be true neutrals.
- Generic AI/SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, glassmorphism, uppercase tracked eyebrows, marketing hype.
- Dashboard clutter: cards everywhere, borders everywhere, stat tiles. This is one surface with one job.

## Design Principles

- **The text is the interface.** Summaries are the product; chrome stays subordinate to set prose.
- **One column, one job.** Input at top, output below, nothing competing for attention.
- **States are designed, not defaulted.** Waiting on a 30s+ model call, errors, and empty results all get deliberate treatment.
- **Editorial, not skeuomorphic.** Book-page restraint (hairlines, pilcrows, italics) without pretending to be paper.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast in both themes, full keyboard operability (including the file drop zone), visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Single user today, but built to standard.
